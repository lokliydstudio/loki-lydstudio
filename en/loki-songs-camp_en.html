LOKI SONGS CAMP

SONGWRITING · COLLABORATION · MUSIC PRODUCTION

The next Loki Songs camp brings artists, producers and songwriters together for a focused week of writing, creating and collaboration at Loki Lydstudio in Bergen.

When

Camp schedule

  • September 16: Travel day / kickoff. In the evening we gather at our regular bar Børskjelleren in the city center to hang out, catch up and get to know each other.
  • September 17: Morning meeting in the studio with group announcements and information. Full day of writing and creating with your group.
  • September 18: Morning meeting in the studio with group announcements and information. Full day of writing and creating with your group.
  • September 19: Morning meeting in the studio with group announcements and information. Full day of writing and creating with your group.
