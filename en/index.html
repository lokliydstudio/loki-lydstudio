ABOUT US

Loki Lydstudio is a modern recording studio in Bergen offering professional services in recording, music production, mixing and mastering. The studio was founded by musicians and producers with a desire to create an inspiring and accessible studio for both new and established artists. We are based at Gyldenpris in Bergen, in facilities designed for efficient and creative music production. The studio consists of several rooms for recording and production, as well as a control room equipped for professional studio work. With us, you can work through the entire process of a song or project — from idea and songwriting to fully produced music.

We work closely with the artists we collaborate with and focus on developing a sound and expression that best suits each project. Loki Lydstudio is run by producers and musicians with experience from studio work, live performances and teaching.

Our goal is to offer a creative working environment where artists can develop their music and achieve professional results.

LOKI SONGS

CREATIVE COLLABORATION · IDEA DEVELOPMENT · MUSIC PRODUCTION

We host songwriting camps for artists, producers and songwriters who want to create new music in an inspiring, focused and professional environment. Our camps bring together creative talent across genres and musical expressions, with an emphasis on collaboration, idea development and the completion of high-quality songs.

At Loki Lydstudio, we provide the framework for productive sessions, efficient workflows and a creative atmosphere where ideas can grow into finished productions. Whether you arrive with a clear concept, a rough idea or want to build something entirely from scratch, we help shape and develop the material further.

Get in touch if you would like to join an upcoming camp, book a private songwriting session or collaborate with us on your next project.
