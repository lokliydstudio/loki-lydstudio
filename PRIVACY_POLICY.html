Loki Songstarter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

What Songstarter stores

When you create an account, Songstarter stores a one-way hashed identifier derived from your Apple account identifier, together with the profile name, artist name, bio and avatar you provide. Content you choose to share can include demos, audio files, lyrics excerpts, chord progressions, tags, comments, likes and private-session content. Safety reports and blocked-user preferences are processed to protect the community.

Cloud storage

Community content is stored with Apple's CloudKit service. Public Feed content can be read by people using the app, including guests. Private sessions use CloudKit Sharing and are available only to invited iCloud participants. Song ideas that you do not share remain locally on your device.

How information is used

Information is used to provide profiles, the Feed, comments, likes, audio playback, private collaboration, safety reports, blocking and account support. Loki Lydstudio does not sell Songstarter personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Moderation and safety

Songstarter screens clearly abusive text before upload. Users can report posts and comments and block other users. Reported content may be reviewed and removed when it violates community rules or applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Shared content remains available until it is deleted by its owner, removed for safety reasons or deleted with the account. You can permanently delete your Songstarter account and associated cloud data from the Me tab. Local song ideas are also removed when you confirm deletion.

Your choices

You may use the songwriting tools and read the public Feed without signing in. You can update your profile, remove your demos, block users, report content and delete your account in the app. Apple controls your iCloud and Sign in with Apple settings.

Contact

For privacy, safety or data-access questions, contact Loki Lydstudio through lokilyd.no and include “Songstarter privacy” in your message.
